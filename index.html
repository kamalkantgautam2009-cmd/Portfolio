01 / FINTECHBCM
PLAKSHA HACKATHON PRAYAS · 3RD PRIZE

Because Credit Matters

An alternative-credit intelligence concept for people with limited traditional credit history. It explores explainable signals such as cash-flow consistency, UPI activity and utility-payment patterns to build a broader picture of creditworthiness.

ALTERNATIVE CREDITEXPLAINABLE UNDERWRITINGTEAM-LED
02 / AGRITECHKS
SMART INDIA HACKATHON · KISANSETU

KisanSetu

A procurement-coordination layer for mandi centres, designed to make queues and counter activity easier to understand. The concept includes rule-based arrival estimates using queue length, counter clearance and delay signals, along with an SMS / IVR flow simulator.

REACT + TYPESCRIPTRULE-BASED ETAMANDI WORKFLOW
03 / HARDWAREVD
SLIET INVENTIA / TECHNI VATION · 1ST PRIZE

VisiDrop

A hardware-software prototype built around a microcontroller and sensor-based interaction. The build brought together a TCRT sensor, LEDs and a buzzer, with a focus on translating a physical input into a clear device response.

MICROCONTROLLERSENSORSPROTOTYPING
04 / UI EXPERIMENTBAD
SSDC HIVEMIND 2026 · WINNER

Bad UI

An eight-level interaction challenge built around intentionally frustrating interface patterns. The idea was to turn familiar usability mistakes into a playful experience and show how strongly thoughtful interaction design shapes a product.
